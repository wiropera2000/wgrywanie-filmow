Wgrywanie filmow

Wgrywanie filmow (“video uploading”) is a private, internal tool used by one independent creator to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels: Gdybologia (Polish) and Illegal There (English).

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files from the creator’s computer to the creator’s YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets the title, description, tags, category, language, audience (“made for kids”) and scheduled publication time.
  • Reads basic channel information to confirm which channel is signed in.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and does not access any other user’s account or data.
